WHAT IS DESIGN BRUT THEN?

Design Brut is a style of design notable for it’s refusal of aesthetic professionals in lieu of pure practical communication. Design Brut has technically existed since the dawn of human communication, however it didn’t exist as an aesthetic until the emergence of the oppositional concept of professional design in the 18th century. Nowadays, these unaesthetic and pure images can be found in the windows of dairies advertising profitable deals, on doors explaining you how to open them and on fences telling the hypothetical delivery man where to hide your parcel.
